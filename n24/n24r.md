## Challenges  
The challenge here was trying to make the language readable through the actual NFA design. Referencing other NFA designs I had drawn out helped to figure out how to make each independent clause before putting it together later on. I did not include the last state for "s4" initially but NFA designs tend to display the shortest path to get accepted so that reminded me to add in the necessary states that this design needed.
